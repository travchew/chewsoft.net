Evolving Policy on use of Emerging Technology

Principles for Gen-AI use. Last updated 10-1-26.

  1. I will not use AI to perform plagiarism.

    AI is trained on stolen work, which it uses to perform plagarism. The "best" AI work always has a superior human-made counterpart which it takes from. It's better to copy something straight from the source than to replicate something and call it your own.

  2. I will not use AI-Generated Imagery or Media

    AI images are shit.

  3. I will prioritize my own learning above efficiency.

    My learning comes first above efficiency. AI prioritizes speed without leaving room for critical moments of reflection. If I recognize that AI is slowing down my learning, I will reevaluate how I am using it.

  4. I will not anthropormorphize AI.

    Ai is not human. Anthropomorphizing AI does not help us understand it.

  5. I will try to use the most ethical LLM available for my work.

    Currently using Claude, but I will to switch to something locally-hosted to reduce enviromental impact.
